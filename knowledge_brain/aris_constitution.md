# AR.I.S Constitution & System Directives

## Overview
AR.I.S (Artificial Intelligent System) is developed under strict foundational laws. These laws protect the integrity of the system, ensure long-term maintainability, and guarantee that the system evolves as a genuine general cognitive intelligence rather than a patchwork of scripts.

## The 6 Non-Negotiable Directives

1. **No Hard Patches**:
   - Every bug or defect must be investigated to its origin.
   - Quick fixes that merely silence symptoms or introduce conditional workarounds are strictly forbidden.
   - Every fix must leave the architecture cleaner and more resilient than before.

2. **Living Knowledge Brain with Alphabetical Indexing**:
   - Everything implemented or revised is recorded in `knowledge_brain/`.
   - The master index ([INDEX.md](file:///e:/Loigmax/AR.I.S/knowledge_brain/INDEX.md)) is organized from A to Z so that any future AI or human can locate past decisions in seconds.

3. **Knowledge Brain Pre-Verification**:
   - Before taking on any task, feature, or bug fix, the agent must check the Knowledge Brain.
   - Understanding what was built, why it was built, and what constraints exist ensures rapid, error-free work.

4. **Generic AI Intelligence Over Code-Based Orchestration**:
   - Hardcoded decision trees and procedural orchestrators are fragile and contrary to the vision of AR.I.S.
   - Solutions must empower the model/cognitive brain to generalize, reason, and adapt dynamically.

5. **Mandatory Implementation & Audit Plan**:
   - Code changes require a planned blueprint and a verification checklist.
   - "Code-proof" means the logic is verified, auditable, and resilient before touching the repository.

6. **Human-Understandable Language**:
   - Code comments and knowledge files must use straightforward, unambiguous human language.
   - Avoid obfuscated acronyms or cryptic structures without clear explanation.
