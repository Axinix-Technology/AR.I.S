# AR.I.S System Vision: The J.A.R.V.I.S.-Scale Roadmap

> **Foundational Milestone Anchor**: AR.I.S is designed to become a true autonomous Artificial Intelligent System comparable to J.A.R.V.I.S. (the fictional Marvel intelligence capable of perception, multi-scale reasoning, autonomous planning, continuous real-world execution, and self-correction).
> **Current Scale**: We are currently building at approximately the **0.5%** foundation level.

---

## Why the 0.5% Level Demands Zero Hard Patches

When building toward an autonomous system capable of reasoning, planning, and executing complex real-world workflows:
- Any shortcut, hardcoded dictionary (`if word in {"who", "what"}`), procedural script, or masked symptom introduced at the 0.5% layer will exponentially destabilize every layer built on top of it.
- A true cognitive intelligence cannot rely on pre-programmed word lists or hardcoded orchestration pipelines. It must discover structure, bind entities dynamically, abstract operational concepts, and formulate its own internal representations.

---

## The Evolutionary Cognitive Pyramid (0% to 100%)

```
[100%] J.A.R.V.I.S. Open-World Autonomy
       - Autonomous multi-agent coordination
       - Metacognitive self-correction & lifelong learning
       - Physical/digital environmental tool execution
       ▲
[ 50%] Autonomous Planning & Multi-Step Execution
       - Goal decomposition & hypothesis testing
       - Dynamic contingency replanning
       - Multi-hop causal reasoning
       ▲
[ 10%] Cognitive Substrate & Operation Learning
       - Structural variable representation (? open slots)
       - Bi-directional relation traversal
       - Relational composition & analogical transfer
       ▲
[0.5%] Current Foundation (Tiers 1 – 5 Primitive)
       - Tier 1: Multi-scale token coordinates & exact span preservation
       - Tier 2: Structural concept & entity candidate extraction
       - Tier 3: Directional relational binding without dictionaries
       - Tier 4: Deterministic graph retrieval & 1-hop traversal
       - Tier 5: Operational schema abstraction & de-lexicalized learning
```

---

## Implications for Current Work

1. **Clean Representation of the Unknown (`?`)**:
   - The reasoner must not "guess" query intent through procedural shortcuts.
   - The query representation itself must structurally encode the open variable slot (`? --R--> Anchor` vs `Anchor --R--> ?`).
2. **Generic Over Hardcoded**:
   - Every mechanism must scale universally across domains, languages, and unseen vocabularies.
   - Never spoon-feed what the brain can discover structurally.
