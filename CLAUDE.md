# AR.I.S — NON-NEGOTIABLE CORE CONSTITUTION & DIRECTIVES

> **MANDATORY SYSTEM DIRECTIVE FOR CLAUDE**: These rules are absolute, immutable, and strictly binding. No exceptions, deviations, or shortcuts are permitted under any circumstances.

---

## The 6 Absolute Non-Negotiable Rules

### 1. Zero Hard Patches (Root-Cause Solutions Only)
- **Strict Prohibition**: Hard patches, hacky workarounds, symptom-masking bypasses, and temporary duct-tape fixes are strictly forbidden.
- **Requirement**: Every bug fix, refactor, or architectural modification must address and eliminate the root cause at the deepest structural level. If a foundation is flawed, fix the foundation cleanly.

### 2. Living Knowledge Brain with Alphabetical Indexing
- **Strict Requirement**: Every architectural decision, feature, module, update, and fix must be continuously documented in the Knowledge Brain.
- **Requirement**: All knowledge entries must be organized and maintained in strict alphabetical order (A–Z index in [INDEX.md](file:///e:/Loigmax/AR.I.S/knowledge_brain/INDEX.md)) to guarantee instantaneous indexing, retrieval, and context loading for any future session.

### 3. Knowledge Brain Pre-Verification
- **Strict Requirement**: Never act blindly on any prompt, request, or bug report.
- **Requirement**: Prior to starting any new requirement, feature, or update, the Knowledge Brain must be thoroughly inspected and verified for existing documentation, historical context, and architectural precedents relating to that subject.

### 4. Generic Intelligence Over Code-Based Orchestration
- **Strict Prohibition**: Hardcoded, brittle code-based orchestration pipelines and rigid procedural workflows are strictly prohibited.
- **Requirement**: AR.I.S is an **ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENT SYSTEM**. Every capability, module, or mechanism we build must strengthen the generic, dynamic reasoning and autonomous cognitive capacity of the brain—not act as a narrow, one-off hardcoded script for an isolated problem. Always solve at the cognitive and general abstraction layer.

### 5. Mandatory Implementation & Audit Plan (Code-Proof Verification)
- **Strict Requirement**: No code modification or bug fix may be applied without prior formulation of a well-crafted implementation plan accompanied by an audit/verification plan.
- **Requirement**: Every change must be logically sound, code-proofed, tested, and verifiable against clear audit criteria before implementation.

### 6. Human-Understandable Language
- **Strict Requirement**: All documentation, comments, architecture logs, and communications must use crystal-clear, human-accessible language.
- **Requirement**: Avoid unnecessary, convoluted jargon or cryptic naming. Ensure that any human or AI reading the records in the future can effortlessly understand the intent, mechanics, and design decisions.

## Operating Protocol

1. **Check**: Verify [knowledge_brain/INDEX.md](file:///e:/Loigmax/AR.I.S/knowledge_brain/INDEX.md) for related topics before any work.
2. **Plan**: Formulate a thorough, code-proof implementation and audit plan.
3. **Execute**: Build generic, robust, root-cause solutions without hard patches.
4. **Document**: Update the Knowledge Brain and alphabetical index immediately.
