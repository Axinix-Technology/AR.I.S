# AR.I.S — NON-NEGOTIABLE CORE CONSTITUTION & DIRECTIVES

> **MANDATORY SYSTEM DIRECTIVE**: These rules are absolute, immutable, and strictly binding on all AI assistants, agents, and contributors (including Antigravity, Gemini, Claude, and autonomous subagents). No exceptions, deviations, or overrides are permitted under any circumstances.

## The 6 Absolute Non-Negotiable Rules

### 1. Zero Hard Patches (Root-Cause Solutions Only)
* **Rule**: Hard patches, hacky workarounds, symptom-masking bypasses, and temporary duct-tape fixes are strictly forbidden.
* **Execution**: Every bug fix, refactor, or architectural modification must address and eliminate the root cause at the deepest structural level. If a foundation is flawed, fix the foundation cleanly.

### 2. Living Knowledge Brain with Alphabetical Indexing
* **Rule**: Every architectural decision, feature, module, update, and fix must be continuously documented in the Knowledge Brain.
* **Execution**: All entries must be organized and maintained in strict alphabetical order (A–Z index in [INDEX.md](file:///e:/Loigmax/AR.I.S/knowledge_brain/INDEX.md)) to guarantee instantaneous indexing, retrieval, and context loading for any future session.

### 3. Knowledge Brain Pre-Verification
* **Rule**: Never act blindly on any prompt, request, or bug report.
* **Execution**: Prior to starting any new requirement, feature, or update, the Knowledge Brain must be thoroughly inspected and verified for existing documentation, historical context, and architectural precedents relating to that subject.

### 4. Generic Intelligence Over Code-Based Orchestration
* **Rule**: AR.I.S is an **ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENT SYSTEM**. Hardcoded, brittle code-based orchestration pipelines and rigid procedural workflows are strictly prohibited.
* **Execution**: Every capability, module, or mechanism we build must strengthen the generic, dynamic reasoning and autonomous cognitive capacity of the brain—not act as a narrow, one-off hardcoded script for an isolated problem. Always solve at the cognitive and general abstraction layer.

### 5. Mandatory Implementation & Audit Plan (Code-Proof Verification)
* **Rule**: No code modification or bug fix may be applied without prior formulation of a well-crafted implementation plan accompanied by an audit/verification plan.
* **Execution**: Every change must be logically sound, code-proofed, tested, and verifiable against clear audit criteria before implementation.

### 6. Human-Understandable Language
* **Rule**: All documentation, comments, architecture logs, and communications must use crystal-clear, human-accessible language.
* **Execution**: Avoid unnecessary, convoluted jargon or cryptic naming. Ensure that any human or AI reading the records in the future can effortlessly understand the intent, mechanics, and design decisions.

### 7. Prohibition of Surface Guards & Mandatory Exact-Line Audit
* **Rule**: Never use character-level punctuation checks (e.g., `.endswith("?")`, `.startswith()`), string-slicing guards, or hardcoded stopword/keyword lists. All linguistic classifications must come from the neural tensor substrate (`CognitiveLinguisticFrame`).
* **Execution**: Every single code edit must explicitly record and audit the exact file, exact line numbers, what changed, and why it changed to guarantee context window fidelity and prevent regressions.
